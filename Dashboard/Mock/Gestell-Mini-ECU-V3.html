🧪 HARDWARE-IN-THE-LOOP (HIL) FAULT INJECTION LAB

Remotely trigger physical sensor errors & test ECU fail-safe reaction latency (< 50ms)
ECU State: RUN OK
F001 FAULT → SAFE MODE
Engine Over-Temperature Injection

Simulates LM35 engine temperature exceeding safety threshold (90.0°C). ECU cuts Fan PWM to 0%, activates 2kHz buzzer alarm, and locks into SAFE MODE.

95.0°C
F002 ELECTRICAL RAIL
Battery Voltage Anomaly Injection

Simulates battery rail dropping below 10.5V or surging above 16.0V. Tests actuator preservation and voltage failure logging.

F003 ANALOG SENSOR
Sensor Disconnection (ADC Saturation)

Simulates ADC pin saturation (0 or 1023 LSB for 3 consecutive 10ms samples), indicating wire cut or short to 5V.

F004 COMMS WATCHDOG
Wireless Telemetry Blackout

Simulates loss of Bluetooth (USART0) or Wi-Fi (USART1) packets for > 10 seconds. Verifies ECU autonomous local operation without reset.
